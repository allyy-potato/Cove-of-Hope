About Us

The story and purpose behind Cove of Hope.

Our story behind the Cove of Hope

We had a friend that was experiencing bullying and he often felt lonely. His friends were leaving him out when they made plans, and that affected him too. Since we were from different countries and both had busy schedules, we couldn't talk much and he would feel unseen and all alone from time to time. He would feel bored and discouraged very often as well.

Our mission

We designed the Cove of Hope, in hopes that people in similar situations have somewhere where they can express their emotions, receive and send encouraging messages with others, experience interactive calming games and have access to support resources.

Our values

Kindness, hope, inclusion and respect.

What we can and cannot do

We can:

Be a private notebook, a kindness wall, a collection of calming prompts, and a bridge toward the people in your life.

We cannot:

Be a therapist, emergency service, crisis line, or replacement for a trusted person who can be with you.

Safety Information

Cove of Hope is designed with privacy in mind.

Your journal entries and drawings are stored locally on your device in this version of the website. They are not sent to our team.

Cove of Hope does not have a public profile system or an inbox. We do not ask users to share personal information to use the activities.

The kindness notes on the Kindness Wall ensures that no negative content is displayed.

Report Something

Use this form to report problems, suggestions, inappropriate messages, or anything else you think should be brought to the attention of the website creator.

Leave a Kind Message
